The pseudo-transient method consists in augmenting the right-hand-side of the target PDE with a pseudo-time derivative (where is the pseudo-time) of the primary variables. We then solve the resulting system of equations with an iterative method. The pseudo-time derivative is then gradually reduced, until the original PDE is solved and the changes in the primary variables are below a preset tolerance.
